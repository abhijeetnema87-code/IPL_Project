§ 01

The compliance complexity

For Axiom's enterprise clients, keeping process documentation current with regulatory change has become slow, expensive, and error-prone — a manual chase that never quite catches up with the rules.

Today's reality

  • New compliance documentation takes months, not weeks, delaying project delivery.
  • Every regulatory shift triggers manual rewriting across fragmented documents.
  • Human error drives accuracy down and audit exposure up.
  • Hundreds of thousands of dollars in lost productivity and potential fines per client.

With ProcessGenius AI

  • First drafts generated from high-level inputs or existing fragmented data — no blank page.
  • Regulatory changes automatically mapped to affected documents, with discrepancies flagged.
  • Audit-ready documentation as a default state, not a scramble.
  • BA time shifts from rework to higher-value advisory work clients already ask for.
§ 02

Four core capabilities

A drafting and flagging assistant embedded in Axiom's existing consulting workflow — not an autonomous decision-maker.

CAP-01

Generate

Detailed, compliant process documentation drafted from high-level inputs or existing fragmented data.

CAP-02

Update & flag

Regulatory changes automatically mapped to affected process documents, with discrepancies flagged for human review.

CAP-03

Explain

Dense compliance clauses translated into clear, natural-language explanations for non-technical stakeholders.

CAP-04

Find gaps

Potential compliance gaps identified across the existing process library, with suggested improvements before they become audit findings.

§ 03

Non-negotiable guardrails

CEO Alex Chen's directive: the AI must never misinterpret a regulation, and human oversight must be absolute for critical outputs.

GATEMandatory human sign-off

Every output routes to a compliance-certified BA who reviews against source regulatory text and explicitly signs off before delivery. Nothing is auto-applied or auto-sent.

TRACESource-linked recommendations

Reviewers see the specific regulatory source behind each AI recommendation — validation happens against the regulation, not the AI's summary.

ROUTEConfidence flagging

Lower-confidence outputs are routed for closer scrutiny, with validation checks built into the pilot from day one.

OWNClear accountability

The signing BA remains the accountable party for anything client-facing — the AI sits inside the existing accountability chain, not above it.

§ 05

Customer research

A 15-question Customer Insight Survey (firmographics → workload & spend → feature and trust priorities → open insights) and a 200-response dataset scoring priorities on a 1–7 importance scale.

What 200 respondents rate most important (mean, 1–7 scale)

Reduced compliance risk6.26
First-draft accuracy of AI output6.11
Fast regulatory turnaround5.92
Urgency to adopt5.88
Ease of integration5.77
Trust in AI output5.59

Risk reduction and accuracy outrank everything else — while trust scores lowest, reinforcing why the human-in-the-loop gate is the product's core design decision, not an afterthought. Source: ProcessGeniusAI_DataSet.csv (n = 200; enterprises 1,000–10,000+ employees).

Survey instrument · 4 sections

  1. About your organization — industry regime (finance / pharma), size, turnover, role in compliance.
  2. Current workload — weeks per document, % time rewriting, accuracy self-rating, annual documentation spend.
  3. Evaluating AI features — capability and trust-&-oversight priorities on 1–7 grids, plus 12-month pilot likelihood.
  4. Your insights — open-text concerns, pain points, and the ideal day-to-day change.

Designed for segmentation: firmographic clusters × workload intensity × feature/trust priority vectors, with qualitative texture to name the clusters.

§ 06

Pilot success criteria

4 months · 3 enterprise finance clients. All four criteria must show meaningful movement — this isn't a single-metric decision.

CRITERION 01

Audit prep time falls

Compliance Audit Preparation Time reduced against the current +30% baseline trend.

CRITERION 02

Accuracy rises

Process Documentation Accuracy Score improves from the 7/10 baseline toward audit-ready by default.

CRITERION 03

Zero critical misreads

No critical compliance misinterpretations reach a final, client-facing document.

CRITERION 04

BA time reallocates

Evidence that analyst time shifts from rework toward advisory work — augmentation, not headcount reduction.
